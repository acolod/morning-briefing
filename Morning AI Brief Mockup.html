For your build journey

Action-oriented curation
  • Use stable lanes for Hermes, agents, dev tooling, and product-building so the brief never loses its center of gravity.
  • Promote recent obsessions into a dedicated module rather than scattering them randomly across the page.
  • Prefer practical interpretation over broad summary: what should be tested this week, what can wait, what is noise?
  • Keep link count low so the brief feels curated rather than dumped.
Recommended posture Use the HTML artifact as the main product, and let Telegram act as the notification and entry point rather than the full reading surface.
