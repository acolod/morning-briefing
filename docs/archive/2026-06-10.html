arXiv ADOPT

Less Context, Better Agents: Efficient Context Engineering for Long-Horizon Tool-Using LLM Agents

This is the most directly actionable item for Hermes. Microsoft tested an MCP-based workflow and found that full conversation retention was both slower and less reliable than keeping only the most recent tool interactions plus automated summaries, with completion rising to 91.6% while runtime and token load dropped sharply. For your setup, the important part is not token thrift by itself; it is that smaller, fresher state beat bloated history on a real tool-using benchmark. I would treat this as evidence to make Hermes context assembly opinionated instead of neutral: recent tool state stays hot, older state gets compressed, and raw long tails stop being the default.

why_now: You are actively building Hermes skills, plugins, gateway, and cron flows, and this gives you a tested MCP-specific context policy you can ship immediately.
